Before you ask

Got questions?

So what is Profits Through Purpose, in one sentence?

We help men take control of their time and their money so they can put it toward the Kingdom of God. The group is where that happens. Coaching is there if you want more one-on-one help.

Do I have to be a Christian?

No. This is built on faith and I won't pretend otherwise, but the door isn't conditional. If you respect the work and the men in the room, you're welcome.

Is this a credit or funding company?

No. Credit and funding are taught inside because they're one way men take control of their time and money. They're tools, not the point. We're not a lender or a credit repair service.

What does it cost?

Right now, nothing. It'll become a paid monthly membership as it grows, but men who come in now keep their founding spot. No card, no trial that quietly bills you.

How much time does it take?

As much as you've got. Courses are self-paced and live calls are recorded. The men who get the most out of it show up to one call a week and post when they're stuck.
