Before you ask

Got questions?

Is this just another guru mastermind?

No, and I'd say the same thing if it were, so judge me on what you see. There's no stage, no chanting, no upsell ladder designed to walk you to a $25,000 room. It's a group of men working on real businesses with somebody who's actually built them. If it starts feeling like a guru thing, tell me and I'll fix it.

How much time does it take?

As much or as little as you've got. The tracks are self-paced. Live calls are recorded. Men in here run companies and work jobs — it's built for that. The guys who get the most out of it show up to one call a week and post when they're stuck. That's it.

What does it cost?

Right now, nothing. It'll become a paid monthly membership as it grows, but men who come in during this stretch keep their founding spot. No card, no trial that quietly bills you.

Do I have to be a Christian?

No. Faith shapes how I run this and I'm not going to pretend otherwise — but the door isn't conditional. If you respect the work and the men in the room, you're welcome. Plenty of guys in here are somewhere in the middle of figuring that out, and that's fine.
