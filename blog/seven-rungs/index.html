The seven rungs: the order that works

Most men skip straight to the money and wonder why it does not hold. The seven rungs from purpose to freedom, and why the order matters.

By Chris Deloach · October 9, 2026

Most men skip straight to the money and wonder why it does not hold. They want funding before they are fundable. They want scale before they have a foundation. They want freedom before they have decided what it is for.

This is the order we walk inside Profits Through Purpose. Seven rungs, one at a time.

1. Purpose

What are you trying to accomplish? Not the revenue number. The reason behind it. Everything below this rung is wasted motion without it.

2. Business model

What should you sell, coach, or build? The business that fits your skills and experience, not whatever is trending this year. Prove it small before you bet big.

3. Foundation

Entity, banking, taxes, bookkeeping. It is not exciting. It decides whether a lender takes you seriously eighteen months from now.

4. Credit

Personal credit cleaned up, business credit built. You can do this yourself. You do not need a company charging you every month to send letters you could send.

5. Funding

Using other people's money, on purpose. Once you are fundable, capital stops being the wall between your idea and the business it could become. Borrow before you are ready and it becomes a weight instead.

6. Scale

Put the capital into something that produces revenue. This is where men either compound or burn it, and where having a room around you matters most.

7. Freedom

Your time back. Your money working. Something worth handing down. And the room to serve the purpose you named on rung one.

Find your rung honestly

Most men are one or two rungs lower than they think. That is not an insult. It is the fastest way forward. Start where you really are and the next step gets obvious.

The door is open.

Everything lives inside the group: the tracks, the live calls, the room. Come in, look around, take the first rung.
