Questions men ask first

Is this a credit repair service?

No. CCD Agency, LLC is not a credit repair organization and does not dispute items on your behalf. The material is educational and do-it-yourself.

Can you remove accurate items from my report?

No, and nobody legally can. You can dispute inaccurate information yourself, for free, directly with the bureaus. You can get your reports at no cost at annualcreditreport.com.

How long does business credit take to build?

It depends on where you start and how consistently you work the steps. Nobody can honestly promise a timeline or a score.
