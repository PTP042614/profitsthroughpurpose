Before You Ask

The questions men actually ask me.

Cost, time, faith, funding, and whether this is for you.

Is this just another guru mastermind?

No, and I'd say the same thing if it were, so judge me on what you see. There's no stage, no chanting, no upsell ladder designed to walk you to a $25,000 room. It's a group of men working on real businesses with somebody who's actually built them. If it starts feeling like a guru thing, tell me and I'll fix it.

How much time does it take?

As much or as little as you've got. The tracks are self-paced. Live calls are recorded. Men in here run companies and work jobs — it's built for that. The guys who get the most out of it show up to one call a week and post when they're stuck. That's it.

What does it cost?

Right now, nothing. It'll become a paid monthly membership as it grows, but men who come in during this stretch keep their founding spot. No card, no trial that quietly bills you.

Do I have to be a Christian?

No. Faith shapes how I run this and I'm not going to pretend otherwise — but the door isn't conditional. If you respect the work and the men in the room, you're welcome. Plenty of guys in here are somewhere in the middle of figuring that out, and that's fine.

I'm at zero. Is it too early for me?

That's rung one, and it's the best place to start. Track 02 is literally about finding what you should build. Most men skip that step, pick something random, and quit eight months later. Starting here saves you those eight months.

Is this a funnel to sell me funding?

Fair question, since I do run a funding business. Here's the honest answer: funding is rung five of seven, and most men who join aren't there yet. If you never use that side of it, the room still works. And if you do get to that rung, you'll get told the truth about whether debt is right for you — including when it isn't.

What if it's not for me?

Then leave, and no hard feelings. I'd rather have thirty of the right men than three thousand of the wrong ones. Nothing to cancel, nobody chasing you with a retention sequence.

The door is open.

Everything lives inside the group: the tracks, the live calls, the room. Come in, look around, take the first rung.
