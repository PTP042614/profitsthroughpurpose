Business Funding

Use capital on purpose. Not out of panic.

Learn your options, get fundable first, and borrow only when the business is ready for it.

Funding is a tool. It is not the point.

Capital can remove the wall between your idea and the business it could become. It can also bury you if you borrow before the business is ready. That is why funding is rung five on the path, after purpose, model, foundation, and credit.

What you learn in the group

  • The main types of business capital and what each one is for: lines of credit, equipment financing, SBA loans, revenue-based financing, and 0% introductory business credit cards
  • What lenders look at and how to prepare before you apply
  • How to use other people's money without putting your household at risk
  • What to do with capital once you have it, so it produces revenue instead of a payment

Questions men ask first

Are you a lender?

No. CCD Agency, LLC and Chris Deloach are not a bank, a lender, or a licensed broker-dealer. Approval, amounts, rates, and terms are decided entirely by third-party lenders.

How much can I get?

Nobody can honestly tell you that up front. It depends on your credit, your revenue, your time in business, and each lender's underwriting.

Should I borrow money for my business?

Sometimes yes, often not yet. Funding is rung five of seven, and most men who join are not there. You will be told the truth about whether debt is right for you, including when it is not.
