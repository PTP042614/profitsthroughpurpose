Life & Purpose Coaching

Find the purpose. Then build around it.

Coaching and brotherhood for men who are doing fine on paper and know there is more.

Doing alright on paper. Hollow in the days.

A lot of men get here. The income is fine. The calendar is full. And there is a feeling that something is off that will not go away. That feeling is a signal, and ignoring it is expensive.

Life and purpose coaching inside Profits Through Purpose is not about hype or morning routines. It is about getting honest on what you want your time and money to serve, then lining your work up behind it.

What we work on

  • Naming what you are building toward. The reason behind the revenue number.
  • An honest audit of your skills, your network, and the advantages you have been overlooking.
  • Self-respect and discipline. Most self-sabotage is not a stress problem. It is a respect problem.
  • Ownership. Controlling what you can control instead of blaming the market, the leads, or the season.
  • Your calendar. Wanting your time back more than you want a bigger number, and building toward that on purpose.
  • Brotherhood. Men who have been through the same fire and will tell you the truth on a Tuesday night.

Chris came from nothing, chased money for the wrong reasons for a long stretch, and changed the whole game when he got tired of the man in the mirror. The businesses came after that change, not before it. That is why purpose is rung one.

How it works

  1. Start with the Find Your Purpose track. An interactive path to the business only you could run.
  2. Set one target for your first 30 days. One. Not seven.
  3. Stay in the room. Weekly accountability and live calls built around men who work for a living.

Chris Deloach founded Profits Through Purpose in 2023 after about 20 years in sales and business: contracting, insurance, and a multi-million dollar agency of his own. Read his story.

Questions men ask first

Is this therapy or counseling?

No. It is coaching and community. It is not counseling, ministry, or a substitute for your pastor, church, or professional support.

Do I have to be a Christian?

No. Faith shapes how Chris runs the group and he does not hide it, but the door is not conditional. If you respect the work and the men in the room, you are welcome.

I do not have a business idea. Is it too early?

That is rung one, and it is the best place to start. The Find Your Purpose track is built for exactly this.

Iron sharpens iron.

Come sit down with men on the same road. Nobody is pretending they have it all figured out.
